Scout Marine Network

A proposed European network connecting Scout Groups from islands and coastal regions connected to Marine Protected Areas — starting from Alonnisos.

Alonnisos, Greece
Marine Protected Areas
European Scout Cooperation
The idea

One island. A European idea.

The 1st Scout System of Alonnisos is proposing the creation of a European Scout network for groups located in or directly connected to Marine Protected Areas.

The network would create a stable space for communication, exchange of experience and joint action between young people working with the sea, their local communities and their protected natural environments.

The initiative is currently at the proposal stage. Alonnisos is the proposed starting point, while future connections with other European areas would be developed step by step.

01

Starting point

Alonnisos and the National Marine Park of Alonissos – Northern Sporades provide the starting point for developing the idea.

The network

What could happen when Scouts connect through the sea?

The proposed network is designed around practical cooperation, youth participation and the exchange of experiences between protected areas.

01

Connect

Bring together Scout Groups from islands and coastal areas connected to Marine Protected Areas.

02

Exchange

Share local experiences, activities, challenges and good practices in marine environmental education.

03

Learn

Discover how different protected areas are managed and how local communities interact with their marine environment.

04

Act

Develop common environmental education and marine protection activities.

05

Meet

Give young people opportunities to connect with Scouts from other European protected areas.

06

Build partnerships

Create opportunities for cooperation with scientists, environmental organisations, management bodies and local authorities.

Proposed first action

Start locally. Share globally.

The first common activity could be simple and meaningful: every participating Scout System carries out an activity in its own Marine Protected Area, documents it and shares the results with the others.

STEP 01

Explore locally

Each Scout Group chooses an activity connected to its own marine environment and protected area.

STEP 02

Document

The young people record what they did, what they learned and what they discovered.

STEP 03

Exchange

The different groups share their experiences and discover how marine protection works in different parts of Europe.

A possible roadmap

From one starting point to a European network.

These are proposed next steps, not completed stages. The network would grow gradually through real contacts and shared activities.

Phase 01

Alonnisos

Develop the concept and establish the initial framework for the network.

Phase 02

First connections

Contact Scout Groups and organisations in other European Marine Protected Areas.

Phase 03

Pilot action

Carry out and exchange the first common marine-related activities.

Phase 04

Grow together

Explore online meetings, youth exchanges, educational activities, camps and wider partnerships.

An idea from Alonnisos, open to Europe.
