Scout Marine Network

A proposed European network connecting Scout Groups from islands and coastal areas connected with Marine Protected Areas, starting from Alonnisos.

Current starting point

Alonnisos

The proposed Marine Network begins with the 1st Scout System of Alonnisos, connected with the National Marine Park of Alonissos – Northern Sporades.

The network has not launched yet. No other Scout Group or location is shown as a participating member at this stage.

The idea

Connecting Scouts through the sea

The proposed network is intended to create a stable space where Scout Groups connected with Marine Protected Areas can communicate, exchange experience and develop common environmental actions.

01 · CONNECT

Meet

Connect Scout Groups from European islands and coastal regions that are directly connected with Marine Protected Areas.

02 · ACT

Protect

Exchange ideas and develop environmental education, awareness and practical activities connected with the marine environment.

03 · SHARE

Learn

Share experiences with other young people, scientists, environmental organisations, local authorities and protected-area management bodies.

The first step

The proposed first common action is simple: each participating Scout System carries out an activity in its own Marine Protected Area, documents the experience and shares the results with the other groups. From there, the network could gradually develop into online meetings, youth exchanges, educational activities, scientific and environmental partnerships, and future transnational meetings or camps.
