New initiative

Scout Marine Network

A proposal to create a European network of Scouts who live, work and learn in and around Marine Protected Areas.

Initiative status
Starting from Alonnisos

The network is currently a proposal under development. Alonnisos is the first and only location shown on the map at this stage.

Europe · Scout Marine Network · 1 location
Starting point

Alonnisos

Alonnisos is the natural starting point of this initiative. The experience of the 1st Alonnisos Scout Group within the National Marine Park of Alonissos and Northern Sporades provides the foundation for the idea of a European network.

01 proposed starting location
2,315 km² approximate area of the National Marine Park

National Marine Park

  • Alonissos and the Northern Sporades
  • Protected marine and terrestrial environment
  • Areas with different protection regimes
  • Environmental education and awareness
What the map shows today

No other European Scout groups or areas are presented as network members yet. The map will grow when real partnerships and confirmed participation are established.

The idea

From one island to a European network.

The proposal is to create a small and meaningful core of Scout Groups from different European countries, connected to Marine Protected Areas.

01 · CONNECT

Meet and learn from each other

Scout Groups from different European regions can connect, exchange experiences and share how the sea influences the life of their local communities.

02 · ACT

Act in our own places

Each participating group could carry out an activity in its own Marine Protected Area, following a shared theme while adapting it to local needs.

03 · SHARE

Share what we learn

Activities, experiences and results can be documented and exchanged, gradually creating a shared body of knowledge and good practice.

The next step

The proposal is to begin small, with a few Scout Groups and clear shared objectives. Over time, the network could develop into a stable European cooperation with joint activities, online meetings, youth exchanges, educational activities and international gatherings.
